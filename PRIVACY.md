# Sprout Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Sprout doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

- **On your device.** Your data and settings are saved on your device only. What the widgets show is shared with the app's own widgets on the same device.
- **Purchases.** Sprout Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The app makes no network connections of its own.

If you have questions, email support@example.com.
