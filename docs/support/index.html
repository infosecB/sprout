Common questions

I bought Sprout Pro but it's locked

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Pro works with Family Sharing.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and search for Sprout. On iOS 18 and later you can also add Sprout to Control Center or the Action button.
