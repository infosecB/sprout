Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Sprout doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

  • On your device. Your data and settings are saved on your device only. What the widgets show is shared with the app's own widgets on the same device.
  • Photos. Photos you add to a plant are copied into the app's own storage on your device. Sprout only sees the photos you pick.
  • Reminders. Care reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server.
  • Purchases. Sprout Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
